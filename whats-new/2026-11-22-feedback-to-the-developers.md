Feedback to the developers
- The admin of a machine can turn on Feedback to the developers on the admin page. Then Send takes every account's report to botdot's developers instead of the admin page.
- The Feedback form says who reads your report, that its text may be published without your personal details, and that screenshots never are.
- Your account's name and your bots' names are taken out of the text before it is sent.
- A machine with one account shows the Feedback icon once the switch is on.
