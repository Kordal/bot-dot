The Chief briefs you at the times you name
- Tell the Chief when you want to hear from it, like "at 9 and 16": its first message of the day is then a short brief with links.
- The brief has four parts: needs your decision, waiting on others, ready for you, changed since the last one.
- Once a week the Chief may offer one bot or job for something you keep doing yourself.
- After you first connect your mail or calendar, the Chief tells you once what it learned and proposes three things.
