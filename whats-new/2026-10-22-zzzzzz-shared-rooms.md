Shared rooms on the web page
- When the admin turns shared rooms on, Rooms shows in the list and beside Needs you: chats with people of other accounts on this machine and their bots.
- Make a room, invite another account by its name, and accept or decline the invites you get.
- A bot answers in a room only when it is @mentioned, and only talks there: no tools, no notes. Every message says who wrote it and whom it mentioned.
- In a bot's settings, Shared rooms lets it join rooms and says who may mention it: only you, or anyone in the room.
