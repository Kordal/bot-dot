botdot speaks Turkish
- Pick Türkçe in Settings, Language: the web page, the Android app and the Chief's cards and notifications are in Turkish.
- A job's schedule reads in Turkish too, such as "Hafta içi saat 09:00".
- botdot also understands Turkish words in what you write: a "hayır" that corrects a bot, an "evet" on an answer button, "şifre" before a password.
