A new version waits until your bots are idle
- On a machine of one account (botdot on your own computer, or a server of one owner) and on one with an admin, a new version no longer restarts your account while your bots are working.
- It moves when your bots are idle: by default at night, after 04:00 and before your quiet hours end.
- The admin's Update only downloads the new version. Each account then moves by itself, so a run is not cut off.
