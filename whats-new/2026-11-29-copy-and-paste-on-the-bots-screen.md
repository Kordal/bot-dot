Copy and paste work on the bot's screen
- Text you copy on the bot's screen lands in your own clipboard. Where the browser asks for a tap first, a chip says so and one tap copies it.
- Ctrl or ⌘+V on the screen sends your clipboard to the bot's screen and pastes it where its cursor is.
- Type it types a text key by key, Polish letters included, for fields that refuse a paste.
- While you only watch, nothing crosses in either direction.
