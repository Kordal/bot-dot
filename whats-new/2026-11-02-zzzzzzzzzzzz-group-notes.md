Groups keep notes of what was settled
- A group, a team's chat too, keeps short notes: decisions, what someone still owes, and facts.
- Every bot in the group reads them in each turn, so nothing settled is lost when the chat moves on.
- botdot adds them as the chat goes on, and the bots can add and remove them too.
- See and change them in the group's settings. A note you wrote or changed stays as you wrote it.
