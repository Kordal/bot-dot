Bots have a new look
- Each bot is now a square in its colour with an icon that shows what it does, in place of the head with a hat.
- A working bot has a turning ring around it; one that needs you or failed has a dot on its corner.
- There are 80 icons to choose from, from invoices and data to travel and pets. Your bots get their icons by themselves within a few minutes. To change one, ask the Chief.
- A bot's square moves once, quietly, when its state changes: a check when a run ends well, a single pulse when it needs you.
