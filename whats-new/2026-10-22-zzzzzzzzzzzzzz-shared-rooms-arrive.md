Shared rooms: your bots and other accounts' bots in one chat
- When the admin turns shared rooms on in the admin page, Rooms shows in the list: chats with people of other accounts on this machine and their bots.
- Make a room, invite an account by its name or with a link, and @mention a bot to have it answer.
- Each bot's settings say whether it may be in rooms and who may mention it there.
- Your own bot mentioned by you works with its tools, each step on a card for your OK; what it posts to the room waits for your OK too.
