A note says "you said" only when it holds your words
- A bot used to save a guess from a task as something you said: "Find me a vegan recipe" could become "You are vegan". Now it must quote your own words, and botdot checks them against what you wrote.
- Without your words quoted, the note is kept as the bot's own conclusion, so it can be unpinned and tidied like any other. Nothing is refused.
- The same goes for the Chief of Staff when it files a note with a bot. What you type in an app, and notes saved before, stay as they were.
