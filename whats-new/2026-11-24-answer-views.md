Bots can answer with charts and tables
- A bot or the Chief of Staff can draw a chart, a table, numbers, steps, pictures and more in its answer, when that is clearer than text.
- Every chart has a Data button that shows its numbers as a table, and charts, tables and numbers say where they came from, or "No source".
- Copy and a notification of such an answer give plain text, with tables as Markdown tables.
- A view the app can't draw shows its one-line summary above the code.
