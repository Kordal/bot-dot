The weekly review settles notes that contradict each other
- When two of a bot's notes cannot both be true, such as two ports for the same server, the weekly review keeps the newer one and removes the other, and says which one it lost to.
- Two notes you said that contradict each other are never changed: the report names them in one line for you to settle.
- One review removes at most a quarter of a bot's notes, and at least 3, so a bad review cannot empty a memory. A removed note can still be brought back for 30 days.
- The Chief's review of its own notes and the shared ones does the same.
