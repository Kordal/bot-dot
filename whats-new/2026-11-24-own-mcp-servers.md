Your own MCP servers
- On the Apps page you can add a remote MCP server of your own, with its address and a key if it needs one, and your bots can use its tools.
- The key is kept encrypted and never shown again, to you, a bot or the AI. Only https addresses on the open internet are accepted.
- A tool that only reads runs at once. Any other waits for your OK on a card, like an app's write.
- Take the server from a bot with Bots…, or remove it for everyone. The Chief of Staff can suggest one, and you add it.
