A click that cannot be undone waits for your OK
- When a bot is about to confirm something a web page says cannot be undone, it asks you first on a card in Needs you.
- Your no is final for that task, also when the bot carries on later; taking control of its screen counts as a no.
- Asking for it in chat is not enough: the card still asks. Pages that only mention the word open no card.
