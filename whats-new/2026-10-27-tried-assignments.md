A bot's new main assignment is tried before you approve it
- When a bot proposes to rewrite its main assignment or rules, it is first tried on its last 20 tasks.
- Its card then says how it did, for example "Better on 14 of 20 past tasks, worse on 1."
- After you approve, "Undo last change" in the bot's settings puts the old text back in one tap.
- The Chief's weekly report lists what changed, with its score.
