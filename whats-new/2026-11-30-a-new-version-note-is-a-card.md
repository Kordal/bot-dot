A new version note is a card
- When a new version of botdot is ready, the Chief's chat shows it as one card: the title, the version, when your account moves to it and a short list of what is new.
- The card has one button, Open card, that takes you to the card in Needs you where Now and Tonight are.
- The Chief's chat in the list says "A new version of botdot is ready".
