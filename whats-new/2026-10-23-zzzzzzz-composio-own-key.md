Connectors use your own Composio key
- botdot no longer makes a Composio account for you: the ones nobody claimed stopped working.
- Connectors ask for an API key from your own Composio project, with a link to get one at composio.dev.
- The key sits at the top of Connectors, on the web page and in the Android app: add, replace or remove it there.
- Without a working key, Connectors show only the key field.
