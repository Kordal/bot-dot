Translations fit their place on screen
- Korean text now breaks between words, not in the middle of a word.
- Capital letters follow your language's rules: in Turkish an “i” becomes “İ”, in a bot's initial, a heading or a label.
- In Japanese, Chinese and Korean, a bot's name inside a sentence is a link to its chat, and a button like “はい、送信” is shown as the yes.
- A schedule in Japanese or Chinese lists its days and times with “、”, as people write them there.
