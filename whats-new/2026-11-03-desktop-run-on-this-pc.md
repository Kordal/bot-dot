botdot on your Windows PC
- The desktop app for Windows can run botdot on the PC itself: Run botdot on this PC, then make your passkey in the browser.
- It sets up WSL if the PC hasn't got it, with one administrator prompt and a restart, and carries on after it.
- botdot runs in a Linux of its own that can't reach your files or your network, and keeps running while you're signed in to Windows.
- Update or remove it from the app's tray menu.
