botdot speaks Japanese
- Pick 日本語 in Settings, Language: the web page, the Android app and the iPhone app show botdot in Japanese.
- Cards, notifications, a job's schedule and What's new come in Japanese too.
- botdot also reads Japanese in what you write: a yes or no on an answer button, "違う" at the start of a correction, "毎日" in a main assignment.
