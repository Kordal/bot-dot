The admin page in groups, with the accounts as a table
- The rail has five groups: Accounts, Usage, Sign-ups, Feedback and Settings. The machine, Update, Shared rooms, Decision model, Replays and boat.dev are under Settings.
- Accounts are a table: type part of a name to find one, click a column to sort, and pick a state to filter.
- Click a row for its share, its update and every action: Change share, Update now, Suspend or Resume, New sign-in link and Delete.
- Disk full, Near its RAM and long commands stay marked in the row.
