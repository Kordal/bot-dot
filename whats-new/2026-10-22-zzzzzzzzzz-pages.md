Pages: reports and overviews your bots keep for you
- Ask a bot for a page, a report or an overview: it saves it as a page, with a chip under its reply that opens it.
- Each bot's pages are listed in its settings, with when they were last updated.
- Tap Keep up to date on a page and pick how often: the bot rewrites it on that schedule, with no card to approve.
- It stays quiet unless something needs you, or tells you every time if you choose so.
