Projects for things that never end
- A new project starts from "What shall we take on?": a goal, an ongoing matter like your home or your car, or a site.
- Something ongoing has no 90 days: no delivery date, no day counter, and a short monthly review instead of the check-ins on days 30, 60 and 90.
- Its goal is what should keep working, and its bots handle the recurring upkeep in their routines.
