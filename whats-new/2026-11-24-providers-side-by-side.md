Claude, DeepSeek, GLM and more at once
- Connect as many model providers as you like beside your Claude subscription or API key. Saving one no longer switches your bots to it.
- In Settings → Model, one list shows every connected provider's models, grouped by provider and searchable. Pick one for the account, the Chief of Staff or any bot.
- A bot runs on the provider of its model. Remove a provider, and its bots run on your default model.
- Each provider's key goes only to that provider, and your Claude sign-in never goes to another.
