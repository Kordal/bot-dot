See what takes space in Settings → Storage
- Settings → Storage shows how much your account's files take, as a bar and a row for each group: your computer, bots' files, projects, uploads, chats and memory, caches.
- Open a group or a folder to see what is big in it, largest first, down to single files.
- Open a folder or file in Files, ask the Chief about it, or delete it after a question on its row; clear the caches in one go.
- The Chief can tell you what takes space too.
