Projects: bot chats docked at the bottom
- Click a bot on the Projects screen and its chat opens at the bottom of the screen, with the project still in view.
- Open more chats side by side; a minimized one is a bar with its unread count, and the rest wait under +N.
- Drag a chat sideways to reorder the row; your open chats are kept on this device.
- On a phone the chat slides up from the bottom; drag it up for full height.
