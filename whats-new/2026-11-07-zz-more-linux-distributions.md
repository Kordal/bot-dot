botdot installs on more Linux distributions
- The installer now sets up Debian 12 and 13, Ubuntu 22.04 and newer, Fedora, RHEL and its rebuilds such as Rocky Linux and AlmaLinux.
- On Fedora and the RHEL family it installs Docker and gVisor with dnf.
- A machine botdot can't run on is told why before anything is installed.
