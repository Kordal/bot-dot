Choose when your account moves to a new version
- Settings has Updates: turn off "Update automatically" and your bots keep working when the admin updates botdot.
- When a new version is ready, Settings says so: tap "Update now" whenever it suits you.
- If you wait, your account moves by itself after the admin's deadline, a week unless the admin set another.
- The admin page shows which accounts wait, and the admin sets the deadline there.
