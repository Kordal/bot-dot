Backups hold your computer's files and restore at any size
- A backup now carries the files on your bots' computer: what they made in their folders and project folders there.
- After a restore those files go back onto the computer as soon as it runs.
- A backup larger than 256 MB restores from Setup: only the free space on the disk limits it.
- The page asks for a passphrase of at least 12 characters, the length botdot takes.
