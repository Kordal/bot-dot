Use your own boat.dev key
- With Advanced on, Settings → Computer takes your own boat.dev key. boat.dev then bills you for the External Virtual PC, and the key is never shown again.
- With your key you choose where your computer is and how big its machine is. Without it, the admin still does.
- A bot can have a machine of its own there, which has costs: choose it in its settings, and copy its files or start empty.
- Removing the key asks whether to delete its machines on boat.dev or keep them. Your bots then work on this machine.
