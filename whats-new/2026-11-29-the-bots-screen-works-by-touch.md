The bot's screen works by touch
- On a phone, tap to click, hold for a right click, drag with one finger, scroll with two and pinch to zoom in and move around.
- A Keyboard button brings up your phone's keyboard, so you can type on the bot's screen.
- A Keys menu sends Ctrl+Alt+Del, Esc, Alt+Tab, Super and Ctrl+L, on any device, while you have control.
