One to-do for the project, shared with your bots
- The panel beside the office floor is now the project's To-do, with All, You, Bots and Done.
- Add a task for yourself or any bot of the project, with a day, and make it repeat daily, weekly, monthly or every few days.
- When you tick a repeating task, the next one appears with its next date.
- The bots of the project and the Chief of Staff see the list too: they add tasks, hand them on and tick off their own.
