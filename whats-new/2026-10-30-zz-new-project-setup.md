A calmer new-project setup
- A new project starts with one field for your site or idea; the clip in it adds files and links.
- One question per screen, with the answer it builds on shown above it.
- The delivery date has its own question: in 1 month, in 3 months, end of year, a date, or Indefinite.
- The review is one SWOT and one list of your answers, each changed with a tap; Delete is in the ⋯ menu.
