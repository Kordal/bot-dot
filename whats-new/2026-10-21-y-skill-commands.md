Run a bot's skill by typing /
- In a bot's chat, type / at the start of the message to see that bot's skills, your Claude account's included, with what each one is for.
- Pick one, or keep typing its name, then write what it should work on: /weekly-report for May.
- The bot runs that skill on the rest of your message. A name it has no skill of is sent as an ordinary message.
