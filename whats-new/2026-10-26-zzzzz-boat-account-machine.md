One boat.dev machine for all your bots there
- Bots on boat.dev now share one machine of the account's, each in a folder of its own, so you pay for one machine instead of one per bot.
- They share its screen and its browser too, as bots on this machine share its computer.
- A bot that must be kept apart can still have a machine of its own: ask the Chief.
- A bot that already had its own machine has its files copied over by itself, and its old machine is let go only after the copy succeeds.
