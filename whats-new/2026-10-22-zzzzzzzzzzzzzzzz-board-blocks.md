The Board looks like a status page
- Each heading says which bots its tiles come from, and you can show one heading at a time.
- A number shows how it changed ("▲ 0.8 since 08:00") with a line of its recent values; charts have a legend, and a tap or hover says the values.
- Switches and buttons under a heading sit together in one Controls box. "Live" at the top says how fresh the newest value is.
- On a phone the tiles go one across, numbers two across. Ask the Chief for a chart, a status or a callout on a tile.
