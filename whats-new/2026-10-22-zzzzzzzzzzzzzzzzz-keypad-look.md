Your bots at a glance, on the home screen
- The Board's values sit over your bots as small live cards with their recent values; on a phone they scroll sideways.
- Needs you is one card: how many things wait, what they are, and Review.
- Each bot shows a status and when it last did something: Working (with a running line), Needs you, unread, Done or Idle.
- Pinned bots are larger. Hotkeys, Esc, Ctrl+K and your order work as before.
