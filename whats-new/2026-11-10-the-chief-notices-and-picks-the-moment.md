The Chief notices, and picks the moment
- A meeting in a project within the hour, a card waiting since yesterday or an overdue to-do no longer waits for the next fixed time: the Chief writes when it matters.
- Every day starts with a brief and ends with a short close of the day.
- Up to 8 messages a day; tell the Chief "less" or "more".
- Nothing comes while you are writing, nor in quiet hours.
