Tables you can sort, search and filter
- A bot's page and the Board can show a table: tap a header to sort, type to search, and pick All, Done or another state to filter.
- States show as coloured pills, and issue and pull request numbers open as links.
- On a phone a table turns into a list, and a long one shows 10 rows a page.
- A table tied to a Board tile stays live and keeps your sort and filter.
