A calendar for the project, shared with your bots
- The office floor has a Calendar: this week, and what comes next.
- Open it for the month. Add an event on any day, with a time if it has one, and move or delete it.
- It also shows the day 30, 60 and 90 check-ins, to-dos with a day, repeating ones included, and your bots' scheduled jobs.
- The project's bots and the Chief of Staff read it and add their own events.
