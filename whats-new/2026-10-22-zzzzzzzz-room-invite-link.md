Invite to a room with a link
- The person who made a shared room can make an invite link in its settings and share it, instead of typing an account's name.
- Opening the link asks to join the room; it works for 7 days, for accounts on this machine.
- "Take back" stops the link at once, and "New link" replaces it.
- On Android, share the link from the room's settings, and paste one under "Join" in Shared rooms.
