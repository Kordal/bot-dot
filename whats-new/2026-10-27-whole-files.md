Files: every file of a bot in one place
- Open Files from a bot's chat or its settings, on the web and on Android. It replaces the Workspace and Files sections.
- Its folder on botdot, on the computer and in each shared project, side by side, as a list or a grid with pictures.
- Select, rename, delete, upload by dragging, and move or copy to any of its places.
- Keep any file on botdot in mind from its menu.
