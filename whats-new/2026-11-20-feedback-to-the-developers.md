Feedback can go to botdot's developers
- The admin of a self-hosted machine can turn on Feedback to the developers on the admin page. Then every account's Send takes the report to them instead of the admin page.
- A machine of one account shows the Feedback icon once it is on.
- The form says that the developers read your report, that its text may be published without personal details and that screenshots never are.
- Your account's name and your bots' names are taken out before a report leaves your machine.
