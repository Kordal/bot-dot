Interactive pages look like botdot
- A bot builds an interactive page from botdot's own style kit: the app's dark colours, Geist, cards, pills, tables and a kanban.
- It starts from a template (plain HTML, Svelte, or a roadmap with views, filters and cards that open) and adapts it.
- Everything comes with botdot, so the page loads nothing from the internet and works on a phone too.
- Bots still prefer a page of blocks; they make an interactive one when you ask for something blocks can't do.
