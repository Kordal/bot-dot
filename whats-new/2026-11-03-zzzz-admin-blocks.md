Block addresses and limit new accounts
- One address makes at most 3 new accounts a day. Change the number under Sign-ups on the admin page.
- Block an address or a range, IPv4 or IPv6, for a day, a week, a month or until you remove it.
- A block stops either new accounts from there, or all access from there. Your own session is never cut off.
- Every block, its removal, a new limit and each refusal are in the Audit log.
