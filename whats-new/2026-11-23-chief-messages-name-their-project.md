The Chief's messages say which project they are about
- A message the Chief writes on its own, or tells you about a bot's result, now shows the project it is about above it. Tap it to open the project.
- A brief or the close of the day names the project on each line, so you can tell at a glance what belongs where.
- You do not see the label in a project's own Chief chat, where the header names it already.
