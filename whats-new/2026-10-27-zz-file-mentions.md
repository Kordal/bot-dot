Mention a file with @ in a bot's chat
- Type @ and part of a name: the bot's files and folders come first, from botdot, its computer and its shared projects.
- Pick a folder, or type / after its name, to look inside it.
- A picked file shows as a chip in your message; tap it to open the file.
- The bot is told where the file is, and gets a small text file's text with it.
