Bots can ask you for input and draw more
- An answer can now hold a form, buttons, sliders, dates, polls and quizzes. What you send comes back as your own plain message, so you see exactly what the bot got.
- A bot can also draw a calendar, a board of cards, an outline, a heatmap, a countdown, an alert and an empty state.
- A button or a form never approves, runs or changes anything: it only sends text. Approvals stay on cards in Needs you.
- Only the newest answer can be answered, once. An older one shows what was sent.
