Saving in the Vault no longer makes you wait
- Adding a login, an SSH key or a file, and choosing which bots may use an item, close their dialog right away while Bitwarden saves.
- If Bitwarden says no, the dialog comes back as you left it, with the reason.
- Sync and Lock, importing skills and duplicating a bot work the same way, with a small line that says how it went.
