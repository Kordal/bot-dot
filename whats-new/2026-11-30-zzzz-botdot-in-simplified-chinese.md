botdot speaks Simplified Chinese
- In Settings, pick 简体中文: the web page, the Android and iPhone apps, the cards and the notifications are then in Simplified Chinese.
- Job schedules, dates and counts are said the Chinese way, such as 每天 09:00 or 9月28日.
- Your bots and the Chief write to you in Simplified Chinese too, unless you write to them in another language.
