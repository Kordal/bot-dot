Bots offer the next step
- When an answer has a date or a deadline, a bot says what to do and when, and offers to take that one step for you.
- Tap Yes and it does it; tap No and it doesn't offer that again in the chat.
- Bots check what they can in your connected apps before they answer.
- A bot can set a reminder: at that time a ⏰ message shows in its chat, with a notification.
