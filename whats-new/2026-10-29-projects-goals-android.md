Projects on Android: goals as pace charts, plan teams and lead reports
- Each goal is a card above the floor: a pace chart, a bar or its status, picked from the card.
- On day 1 a goal shows its target, the day and the first check-in date instead of an empty chart.
- Each item of the week's plan shows the team that does it.
- A team's sheet has Reports, how often its lead reports, and the latest report.
