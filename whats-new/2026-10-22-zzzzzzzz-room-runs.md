Your own bot can work for you in a shared room
- When you @mention your own bot in a room, it takes it as a message in its own chat, with its tools, notes and computer.
- Every tool it uses there waits for your OK on a card in Needs you, one at a time. Nobody else in the room sees the cards.
- Its reply goes to the room only after you post it from its card.
- A bot in a room can also write there itself with post_to_room, from a job or a call too, and each message waits for your OK first.
