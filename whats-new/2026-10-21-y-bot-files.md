A bot remembers the files you gave it
- Every task of a bot now lists the files you gave it, and it can always read them, even without the Read permission.
- "Keep in mind" a file (a CV, a price list): its text, or the bot's own summary of it, comes with every task.
- Bot settings have a Files section: open, keep in mind or delete each file. You can also tell the bot, or ask the Chief.
