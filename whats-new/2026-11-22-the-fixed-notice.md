Hear when your report is fixed
- Once your account runs the update that fixed a report, botdot tells you once: "Fixed: your report from 3 Nov", with the first words of what you wrote.
- It never says so before your account runs the fix. If you postponed an update, the notice waits until you switch.
- Closed in one app, it is gone in all of them, on the web page and in the Android app.
