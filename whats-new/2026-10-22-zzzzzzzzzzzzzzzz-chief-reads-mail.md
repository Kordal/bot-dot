The Chief notices what's coming in your mail and calendar
- When Gmail, Google Calendar or Outlook is connected, the Chief's own messages also look at your next 48 hours: a check-in that opens, a ticket that hasn't come, an invitation you haven't answered.
- It only reads: it never sends, changes or deletes anything there, and asks you nothing on a card.
- If it can help, it offers one step with Yes and No buttons.
