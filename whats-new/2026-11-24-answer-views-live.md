Numbers in an answer can stay live
- A bot can bind a number, a gauge, a chart or a table in its answer to a tile of your Board, and it then updates in the open chat without the bot running again.
- The tile is the source: its logins, its script and your OK on it, and its refresh. A view adds none of its own, and shows the tile's name as the source.
- Copy, notifications and rooms keep the numbers the bot wrote. If the tile is gone or can't be read, the answer simply shows them.
