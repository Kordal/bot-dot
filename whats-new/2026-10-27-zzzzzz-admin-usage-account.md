One account's usage on the admin page
- Click an account in the Usage table to open its own numbers below it: runs a day over 30 days, outcomes, failures and features.
- Each failed run is listed: when, why (ran over 4 h, Claude usage limit, timed out…), which bot by number, from a chat or a job, how long it took and which tools failed.
- It also shows active days, cards answered and the typical answer time, web vs Android, activation, week 1 and week 4, and its version.
