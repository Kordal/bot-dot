Several questions in one view are sent together
- A view with more than one quiz or poll now lets you pick an answer to each, change your mind, and send them all with one Send.
- Each quiz then shows whether your own answer was right.
- A question that ends in "?" now reads "How was your week? 8" in what you send, with no colon.
- A chart's "No source" shows when a bot writes "none" or "n/a" as the source, and a row of three numbers no longer leaves one alone on its line.
