Interactive pages
- A bot that uses the computer can publish a small site it built as a page: views to switch, filters, a kanban, cards that open.
- It runs isolated on an address of its own: it can't see your account or reach the internet, and a link out asks you first.
- Versions lets you go back to an older one; Download gives you its files as a zip.
- After an update, port 8443 carries these pages; on a public domain keep it open.
