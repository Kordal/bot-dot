A project update is a card
- The update from your Project Manager or the Chief shows as one card: the project, the period, and pills for what needs you, blockers, what is done and what is in progress.
- It starts folded to its head and pills. Tap it to see only the sections that have something: what needs you, blockers, goals, done, in progress, decisions and the latest replies.
- Open card, Open chat and each row take you to where the answer is. The bots list says the outcome, for example "Nothing needs you, 2 in progress".
