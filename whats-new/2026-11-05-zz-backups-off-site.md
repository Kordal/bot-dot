Backups go off-site, and you download one when you want it
- Settings → Backups shows when your account was last backed up off-site, together with the machine, every night.
- Download makes a backup of your account now, encrypted with a passphrase you choose for it, and nothing of it stays on the server.
- The nightly backups your account kept on the server are removed once it is backed up off-site, which frees their room.
- Restore a downloaded backup as before, from Restore a backup on the setup page, on this server or another.
