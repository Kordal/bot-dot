Shared rooms in the Android app
- When the admin turns shared rooms on, "Shared rooms" in the list opens your rooms and the invitations waiting for you.
- In a room every message says who wrote it, "name · account" for a bot, and whom it mentioned; type @ to pick a bot.
- Make a room, invite another account by its name, add or remove your bots, leave it, or delete one you made.
- A bot's settings say whether it may be in shared rooms and who may mention it there.
