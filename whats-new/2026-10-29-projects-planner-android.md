Projects on Android: To-do, calendar, standup and decisions
- The To-do under the office floor has All, You, Bots and Done, and a task gets a bot, a day and a repeat.
- The Calendar shows this week on the floor; tap it for the month, and add, move or delete an event.
- The morning standup is one card, with Reply into the lead's chat when a team waits for you.
- Your last decisions sit under the To-do: type one, with "because" and the reason.
