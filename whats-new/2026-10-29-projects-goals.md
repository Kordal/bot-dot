Goals as pace charts
- Each goal with a number shows as a chart: where it stands against the line to its target over the 90 days.
- Pick how a goal shows from its card: pace chart, bar or status. A goal with no number shows its steps to tick.
- On day 1 a goal shows its target, the day and the first check-in date instead of an empty chart.
- The floor keeps the teams in the main area; the Chief, bots without a team and New team sit in a narrow column.
