A decisions log for each project
- Projects keeps your decisions with the reason and the day, under You: type one, such as "No paid ads because it is too early".
- Tell the Chief of Staff or a team's lead a decision in chat and it writes it down for you.
- Your answer at a day 30, 60 or 90 check-in is logged too.
- The project's bots read the latest decisions in every run, so they stop asking what you settled.
