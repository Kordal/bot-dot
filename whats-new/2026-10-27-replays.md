The Chief tests a skill before proposing it
- A skill the Chief's weekly review proposes is first tried on the bot's last 20 tasks, with and without it.
- Its card says how it did, for example "Better on 14 of 20 past tasks, worse on 1."
- A skill that did worse gets no card; the review's report names it.
- The machine's admin turns this on with "Replays an hour".
