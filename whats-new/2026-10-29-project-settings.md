Project settings
- The gear beside a project's name opens its settings: name, colour, goal and objectives, teams and leads, reports, the morning standup and the rules.
- Every change is saved as you make it, and changes from the Chief show at once.
- Archive a project to move it under Archived at the end of the list; botdot starts nothing for it until you bring it back.
- Delete removes a project after you confirm. Its bots stay.
