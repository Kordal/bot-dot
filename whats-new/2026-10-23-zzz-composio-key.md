Connectors say plainly when Composio refuses the key
- When Composio stops accepting the key, the Connectors page says whose key it is: botdot's own Composio account or yours.
- One step fixes it: New Composio account (botdot and your bots stay as they are), or paste a new key of your own.
- The apps you had show with Connect again, and bots get them back once connected.
- botdot first fetches the newest key of its own Composio account, so a replaced key keeps working by itself.
