Bots save secrets in the Vault, with your OK
- A bot that made a password or an app secret, say on a server it set up, can now keep it in the Vault.
- It asks on a card: the entry's name, its address and where the secret comes from. Nothing is saved until you tap Save.
- Show on the card lets you see the secret first. It never appears in the bot's chat.
- Show and Hide also sit beside every secret you type in the Vault.
