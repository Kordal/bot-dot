## Description (4000)

```
botdot is the iPhone app for your botdot: a team of AI bots built on Claude Code, which you run by chat.

You don't configure bots by hand. You tell the Chief of Staff what you need, and it makes the bots, runs them and reports back. Each bot has one job, its own tools, permissions, workspace and schedule.

What you can do in the app
• Chat with each bot and with the Chief of Staff, and share files, photos, links and text into a chat from any app.
• See what needs you: approve or decline what a bot asks before it acts.
• Watch your Board: live values your bots keep up to date, such as unread mail, your next meeting or a room's temperature.
• Put two to six bots in a group: write once, and they answer and hand the work on to each other.
• Projects: a team of bots, their files and their chat, in one place.
• Get a notification when a bot replies, finishes or needs you. Its text is sealed for your iPhone alone.

You need a botdot account
The app connects to a botdot server and shows nothing without one: your own (botdot is free and open source, and installs on a Linux server with one command) or an account a botdot machine gives you. Your bots work with your own Claude subscription.

Private by design
The app talks only to the botdot you sign in to. No analytics, no ads, no tracking. Sign-in uses a passkey on your botdot's own page.
```
