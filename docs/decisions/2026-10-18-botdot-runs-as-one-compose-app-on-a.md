2026-10-18: botdot runs as one Compose app on a Docker host with its own reverse proxy (`compose.single.yaml`, docs/install-docker.md): one account made with a setup key, named volumes, no published port, and the front door takes the client from the last `X-Forwarded-For` address when `FRONT_DOOR_BEHIND_PROXY=1`, because the owner wants botdot on a Dokploy VPS, where Traefik owns ports 80 and 443 and `install.sh` would want the machine to itself. It runs on the Linux platform with `BOT_DOT_ACCOUNT` set, so its updater stays off and a redeploy updates it. A one-shot service gives the front door's new volume to its user, and `compose.single.caddy.yaml` adds Caddy for a host without a proxy of its own. Rejected: `compose.mac.yaml` as it is (it says it runs on a Mac and binds folders a redeploy may wipe), and trusting `X-Forwarded-For` only from private addresses (the front door publishes no port here, so only the proxy reaches it).
