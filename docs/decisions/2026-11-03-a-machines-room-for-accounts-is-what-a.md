2026-11-03 (#912): A machine's room for accounts is what a new account takes at once (3 GB of RAM: idle and its Chief replying; 10 GB of disk: its images, 7.2 GB, and what its containers write; ADR 028) against what the machine has free now, not the sum of the shares the ticket named. Shares are ceilings that overlap by design: the default RAM share is half the machine's (#126) and the default disk share a sparse 30 GB image, so their sum called a machine of one or two accounts full (`make install-real` on a 16 GB runner refused the second account). Room can rise and fall with the load, which the full page's "try it again later" fits. A refused sign-up is an audit entry once an hour for each link, so that a page opened again and again doesn't fill the log.
