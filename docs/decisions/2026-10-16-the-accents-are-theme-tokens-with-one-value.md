2026-10-16: The accents are theme tokens with one value in light and dark: `dot` `#d85a30`, `mention` `#378add` and `warn-line` `#ef9f27` (`--warn-line` on the web page, `warnLine` in Android's `Paper` and the Mac's `Theme`). `tests/theme-parity.test.js` reads the three theme files and fails when a colour token is missing in one app or has another light or dark value; `npm test` runs `tests/*.test.js` too, and CI tests the server when Android's `ui/Theme.kt` alone changed, so a one-app change is caught. The Mac now has `warn` (`#b27a12`, dark `#e2a846`) like the other two: its tiles' and its sidebar's warn dots take it, words keep `warnText`. The Chief's colour (`CHIEF_COLOR`, `Who.chief.color`), the bots' palette and the icons keep their own literals: they are data and pictures, not the theme. Android's launcher background is `#000000`, Mono's black. ([#447](https://github.com/Kordal/bot-dot/issues/447))
