2026-10-27: Files (#603) carries a copy or move between botdot and the computer as one tar of plain files and folders, at most 25 MB, packed and unpacked as `box`, not file by file: it lands whole or not at all, and a move takes it from where it was only after that. Links are left out of what is carried, so a move of a folder holding one is refused (`has-links`) rather than losing them. Folders pinned to the side are kept per browser, not on the server; names starting with a dot are listed with the rest, since a bot's `.env` is its owner's to see.
