2026-10-22: A bot's shared-room settings (#316, ADR 060) are one per bot, `rooms: { allowRooms, whoMayMention }`, not one per room: the first version needs only "may it be in rooms" and "who may mention it", and a per-room override can be added beside them later. Only the owner changes them (the Chief's or a bot's change is 403, not a card): they decide who may spend the owner's Claude use, and the settings screen is where the owner sets them. `GET /api/room-bots` gives the front door only the bots with rooms on, so nothing of the others leaves the account. ([#316](https://github.com/Kordal/bot-dot/issues/316))
