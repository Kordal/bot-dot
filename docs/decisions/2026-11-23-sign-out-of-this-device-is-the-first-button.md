2026-11-23: Settings → Sign-ins has "Sign out of this device" (`POST /sign-out`, this session only) as its primary button and "Sign out everywhere" (#468) after it, on the web page and in the Android app: most owners who sign out mean this device, and the wider one is the answer to a lost or stolen one. The label says "device", not "browser", because the same button is in the desktop app and on the phone, and it does not say "Sign out" alone because Settings → Claude has a "Sign out" of its own.
