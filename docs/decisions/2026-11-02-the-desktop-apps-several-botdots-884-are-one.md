2026-11-02: The desktop app's several botdots (#884) are one list in its `session.json`, each address with its own `Set-Cookie`, and the file from before (one `address`, one `cookie`) is read as a list of one. The app's controls are menus only: the tray icon's on Windows (a click shows the window, the menu is on the right button), the menu bar icon's and the app menu (under About) on the Mac, with no window menu on Windows. A botdot is switched by going through the app's own screen, which enters it with its session, and Remove acts on the one shown. "Open at login" uses `tauri-plugin-autostart` (the user's Run key on Windows, a LaunchAgent on the Mac), is turned on once at the first start of a built app and never again, and starts the app with `--hidden`, in the tray with no window. The menus' words follow the system's language, through `sys-locale`.
