2026-11-04: A chat that stayed on screen while another window was in front gets its "New" line on return but no scroll to it (`syncRead`, `lineKeeper.arrive(…, scroll)`): the replies came in sight, and the click that brings the window back (often on a suggested answer) moved the chat up under the pointer and off its end, so later replies stopped following. A chat that comes on screen, or a page that was hidden, still scrolls to its line.
