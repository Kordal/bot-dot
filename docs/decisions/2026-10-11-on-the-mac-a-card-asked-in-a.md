2026-10-11: On the Mac a card asked in a group takes its group from the card itself (`groupId` on an ask, a login request, a memory change) and, for a job or a tile, from the feed's `approvals`, as the Android app does; the `cards` list on the group's chat is not read. The notification of a login or payment asked in a group opens the group, not its pop-up, as docs/api.md says. A chip under a reply in a group opens the bot's jobs or settings as it does in the bot's own chat (Android's opens nothing there), because one view draws both replies. (#374)
