2026-10-19: A bot's screen on the account's computer ends after 30 minutes unused (`BOT_DOT_SCREEN_REST_MS`): its desktop, its X server and its Camoufox stop, and its browser profile and files stay. Its next computer tool starts it again. A call still running and the owner watching the screen both count as use. A screen that starts again first ends what is left of its last start, so no second desktop runs beside it. On hq one computer held three desktops (about 0.5 GB each) and Camoufox (about 2.4 GB). Under gVisor that memory is the sandbox's own: the account can't reclaim it, and it grew about 1.1 GB an hour until the account hit its share and thrashed the disk. Rejected: a memory limit on the computer (`--memory` isn't enforced under `--ignore-cgroups`, and the guest still sees all of the machine's RAM), and restarting the whole computer at night (it would end every bot's work). ([#126](https://github.com/Kordal/bot-dot/issues/126))
