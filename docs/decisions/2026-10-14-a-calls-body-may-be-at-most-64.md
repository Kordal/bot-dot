2026-10-14: A call's body may be at most 64 KB (`BODY_MAX` in `lib/calls.js`): enough for any event a home device sends, and small enough to keep in a prompt and on a queue item. A call to a job without a key, or to a job that does not exist, answers `401` as a wrong key does, so a sender without the key learns nothing of the jobs. ([#407](https://github.com/Kordal/bot-dot/issues/407))
