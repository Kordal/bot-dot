2026-11-06: On Android, Send (Stop on an older botdot) is a 48 dp tap target drawn as its 36 dp circle, laid out so the message box keeps its height (#1113). The round buttons (`RoundButton`, 32 to 40 dp, in 40 places) keep their drawn size and reserve no 48 dp of layout: Compose already widens the tap of a clickable smaller than 48 dp to 48 dp unless a neighbour is nearer, so a finger finds them, and reserving the space would move every header and row they stand in (the narrow list's three 34 dp buttons would no longer fit in 140 dp). Rejected: `minimumInteractiveComponentSize()` in `RoundButton` for that reason.
