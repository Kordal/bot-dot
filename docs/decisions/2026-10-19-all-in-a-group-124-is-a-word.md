2026-10-19: "@all" in a group (#124) is a word at the start of the owner's message, not a member, a setting or a button: the message records `together: true`, every member's turn is given at once and their replies hand on to nobody; without it the turns stay one after another (ADR 019, amended). `@all` is offered by the mention suggestions only at the start of a message, where it works, and only when the server announces `group-all`. Rejected: a toggle by the message box (a new control for one word, AGENTS.md rule 5) and `@all` anywhere in the text (a reply quoting it, or a sentence about it, would send to everyone). ([#124](https://github.com/Kordal/bot-dot/issues/124))
