2026-10-23: The style kit and templates of interactive pages (#400) ship in botdot's image with the `kit:pages` skill (`bot-skills/skills/pages/site-kit/`), and `start_interactive_page` copies one into the bot's computer as a tar the box user unpacks, rather than baking them into the desktop image: a kept computer runs on the image it was made from (#191), so the kit would reach it only after a reset. The fonts are the web page's own (`public/fonts`), added when the tar is made, not a second copy. The Svelte template pins Vite, Svelte and its plugin with a lockfile; the bot's computer fetches them with `npm ci`.
