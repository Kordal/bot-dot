2026-10-16: The front door counts calls with a wrong key in a count of their own, apart from sign-ins, with the same numbers (5 free, then twice as long each time up to 15 minutes): every `401` of a call counts, an account that does not exist answers and counts as a wrong key, and a call let in forgets the count. A call let in forgets nothing (unlike a sign-in), so that the key of one job can't clear the guesses at another's; an hour without a wrong key does. Rejected: one count for both, which would let a sender set up wrong lock its owner out of signing in from the same address. ([#472](https://github.com/Kordal/bot-dot/issues/472))
