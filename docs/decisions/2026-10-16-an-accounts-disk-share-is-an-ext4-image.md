2026-10-16: An account's disk share is an ext4 image of its size (`/var/lib/botdot/disks/<name>.img`, sparse, made with `-m 0`), loop-mounted as its home before its Linux user is made, and by its unit (`ExecStartPre=+…mount -o loop`) after a restart. A bigger share grows it online (`truncate`, `losetup -c`, `resize2fs`); a smaller one is not applied to it, because ext4 doesn't shrink while mounted, and applies to accounts made from then on. An account whose home was made before disk shares keeps it, without a disk of its own, and the installer says so. Rejected: project quota (ext4 on the machines botdot runs on has it off, and turning it on needs a remount of `/`), and a per-account filesystem on LVM (not every machine has a volume group). ([#471](https://github.com/Kordal/bot-dot/issues/471))
