2026-10-19: The account's computer has one Tailscale device (#106, ADR 035), run by root in userspace (`--tun=userspace-networking`), not in kernel mode: kernel mode needs `NET_ADMIN` and `/dev/net/tun` in the container, and with `NET_ADMIN` root, which every bot is through sudo, could change the firewall that keeps botdot's control and screen ports from the tailnet and the local network. `botdot-tailscale` starts itself again through `sudo -n` when a bot runs it as box, keeps Tailscale and its state in `/var/lib/botdot-tailscale` (mode 700), and on `resume`, which botdot runs as root at each start of the computer, sets `--shields-up=true` again. Because userspace Tailscale hands a tailnet connection on to `127.0.0.1`, the firewall drops TCP that root sends on the loopback to botdot's ports (`-m owner --uid-owner 0`); nothing of botdot's connects to them as root. A Docker runtime without the owner match keeps the rest of the firewall, and botdot logs it. A bot's userspace Tailscale in its old computer or in `/home/box/.local/tailscale` is not carried over. Rejected: one device per bot (a tailscaled each, on proxies of their own, for no wall between them), and the owner match as a reason to refuse the computer's start (Tailscale's shields still hold).
