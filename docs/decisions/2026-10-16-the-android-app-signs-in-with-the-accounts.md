2026-10-16: The Android app signs in with the account's passkey through Credential Manager (`androidx.credentials`, with Google Play services for Android 13 and older), and the front door takes the app's origin, `android:apk-key-hash:…`, for the signing certificates named in `FRONT_DOOR_ANDROID_KEYS` and serves them as `/.well-known/assetlinks.json`. Android lets an app use a site's passkeys only when the site's asset links name the app. The session's secret is kept in the app's private preferences (Android never backs the app up), one per botdot address, and sent as the cookie by an OkHttp interceptor, so pictures and the live feed carry it too; a bot's screen gets it in the WebView's cookies. Rejected: OkHttp's `CookieJar` (it would keep every cookie any server sets, where the app needs one). (#468)
