2026-11-12: a managed machine's default share is 6 CPUs, 9 GB of RAM and 150 GB of disk, up from ADR 154's 4, 8 and 40. A managed machine holds one customer, so its one account gets all a Contabo Cloud VPS 6 with its 200 GB SSD can give: its 6 CPUs; 9 GB, the most that fits its measured 11.7 GB beside the 2 GB reserve (10 GB would make the install refuse the machine); and 150 GB of its disk (about 186 GiB), leaving the rest for the system and the images. The 100 GB NVMe variant has no room for that disk. A Cloud VPS 8 now also holds one account; a machine of several needs 12 CPUs and 20 GB for two. An account with no share of its own takes the new default at root's next step after the update: its CPU and RAM limits are set again and its disk grows.
