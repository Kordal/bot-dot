2026-10-20: The update's room check counts the new server image's layers an account lacks three times over (the download, then unpacked at about twice its size: botdot's image is about 1.5 GB to download and 4 GB on disk) and 1 GB more, so the account can go on working after it. The sizes come from the signed image's manifest and its configuration's `diff_ids`, the ids `docker image inspect` gives as `RootFS.Layers`, read by `lib/image-layers.js` on the Node that checks the signature. Only the server image is counted: the computer image's download may fail without stopping the update. Rejected: a fixed size per update (a small update would stop on a nearly full disk, a large one would pass and fail), and reading the layer's unpacked size from the registry (it doesn't give it). ([#192](https://github.com/Kordal/bot-dot/issues/192))
