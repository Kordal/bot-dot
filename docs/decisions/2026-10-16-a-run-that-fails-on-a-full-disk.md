2026-10-16: A run that fails on a full disk says "No space left on the server." and nothing of a size or a limit (AGENTS.md rule 5): botdot reads it from "no space left on device" (`ENOSPC`) in what failed, or from less than 64 MB free on its data's disk when a run fails. An account's botdot keeps a 32 MB spare file in its data, removed when a run fails on a full disk so that its record and the next messages can be written, and made again after a run that ends well; the admin page marks an account whose disk has less than 200 MB free "Disk full". The default share is 2 CPUs, 5 GB of RAM, 2 computers and 30 GB of disk: enough for one bot's computer (its desktop wants about 2 GB) beside the botdot server, and 4 accounts on a machine of 8 CPUs and 32 GB.
