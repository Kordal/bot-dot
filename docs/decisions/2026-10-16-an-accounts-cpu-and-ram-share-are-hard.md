2026-10-16: An account's CPU and RAM share are hard limits on its systemd unit (`systemctl set-property botdot-account@<name>.service CPUQuota= MemoryHigh= MemoryMax=`), applied at once and kept by systemd in `/etc/systemd/system.control`, because runsc runs with `--ignore-cgroups` and the unit's cgroup holds the whole rootless Docker. `MemoryHigh` is 90% of `MemoryMax`, so the account's work slows down near its RAM before the kernel ends anything at it. Without systemd (a container) they aren't applied, and the installer says so. Rejected: Docker's own `--cpus`/`--memory` per container (an account starts its own containers, and gVisor ignores them). ([#471](https://github.com/Kordal/bot-dot/issues/471))
