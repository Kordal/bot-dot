2026-10-20: A diagram on the web page is as wide as Mermaid drew it (its `viewBox`) and at most as wide as the bubble, set on the drawing itself, because Mermaid gives its SVG `width="100%"` and an inline `max-width`, which make it a few pixels wide in a bubble as wide as its words and let it overflow a narrow one. On Android "Open on web" is a link of the text (`LinkAnnotation.Url`), as the links of a reply are, which opens the browser as `Intent.ACTION_VIEW` does.
