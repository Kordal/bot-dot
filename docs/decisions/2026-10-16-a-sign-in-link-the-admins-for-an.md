2026-10-16: A sign-in link (the admin's, for an owner who lost every device) is kept with the sign-up links (`signIn: true`, at `/sign-up/<token>`, listed and revoked with them), works once and ends after 1 day, not 7, because it opens an account that holds data. Its passkey is made with the account's own WebAuthn user handle; using it ends every session of the account (the lost devices') and keeps the account's other passkeys, which may be on a device the owner finds again. Rejected: removing the old passkeys (a link handed to the wrong person would then lock the owner out as well as let them in). A suspended account keeps its sessions, so that resume brings it back as it was; its owner can still sign in, and gets the paused answer. ([#470](https://github.com/Kordal/bot-dot/issues/470))
