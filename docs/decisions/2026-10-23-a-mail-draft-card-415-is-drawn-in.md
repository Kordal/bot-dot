2026-10-23: A mail draft card (#415) is drawn in the Chief's chat only: a bot's draft reaches the owner through the Chief, which writes it as the `[[draft]]` block, so the bots' own chats keep their answer buttons. A sent mail is a `[[draft sent]]` block with To and Subject only, and botdot copies the text from the version it sent, so the Chief never writes the mail twice. Its states are set by botdot (Not now marks it kept; a newer version with the same To and Subject folds it), not worked out by the apps. ([#415](https://github.com/Kordal/bot-dot/issues/415))
