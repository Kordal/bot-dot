2026-11-04: A managed machine's room counts a reserve of 2 GB of RAM for the system and none of CPUs, against #983's "at least 2 GB of RAM and 1 CPU": a share's CPUs are a quota of CPU time (`CPUQuota`), not CPUs of its own, so the system runs in what the accounts leave idle, and ADR 154's Cloud VPS 8 (8 CPUs) holds two shares of 4 only so. Its several-account variant names its first account in `FRONT_DOOR_FIRST_ACCOUNT` and keeps `FRONT_DOOR_ADMIN` in `.env` for the installer alone (the front door ignores it there): the installer guards that account from an erase by it. The accounts counted when a machine goes back to one are its account folders and the front door's pending requests, not the front door's data, which needs the image's Node before any image is downloaded ([#983](https://github.com/Kordal/bot-dot/issues/983)).
