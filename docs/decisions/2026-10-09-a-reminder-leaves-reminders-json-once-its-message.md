2026-10-09: A reminder leaves `reminders.json` once its ⏰ message is in its chat file, because removing it first lost it, and every later one of the same run, whenever its delivery failed. That is after the write for a chat with no Chief turn going on, and after the turn's save for one with a turn (the reminders handed to a turn are kept on the turn, so a turn that never ends blocks none past a restart). One that fails is logged as `[reminders]` and tried again on every 15-second run, with no limit and nothing shown in the app, and a feed that could not be told afterwards is only logged. One posted whose removal could not be saved is kept in memory as posted: it is not posted again, and only its removal is tried again (a restart forgets that, so at worst it goes off once more then). Rejected: removing it before delivery (a failure lost it, and the rest of the run); retrying after a failed feed event (it would post the ⏰ message twice); a limit on attempts (nothing asked for one).
