2026-10-10: A Chief turn whose save at its end fails still ends: the chat is marked idle and what waited behind it starts, and the chat (the reply and the messages that waited in the turn, its reminders included) is kept in memory and saved again on the reminders' 15-second check until that works. Until then `readChat` hands out a copy of that chat, so what changes the chat meanwhile (the next turn, a bot's line) keeps them and its own save is the one that works; a reminder among them leaves `reminders.json` only then. Each step after the save (the feed, `done`, the waiting turns) is caught and logged on its own, and none saves the chat again. The failure is only logged as `[chief]`; nothing new is shown in the chat or the feed, and a look's record and a run's state stay as they are. A restart before the save works forgets them, as it does a posted reminder: the reply shows as cut off. Rejected: a timer of its own (one check for both, as the owner chose); keeping the chat busy until it is saved (the owner could not write there, and what waited behind it would not start); writing the messages to another file (a second place a restart has to read).
