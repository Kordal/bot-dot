2026-10-21: A bot whose own rule stops what the owner asked asks to lift it (`ask_override`, ADR 052) for one action, not for the rule as a whole: "Allow once" keeps nothing, "Allow always for this bot" keeps the rule and that action until the owner revokes it, at most 10 per bot. The lifted rules go in the run's task, not the system prompt, which is near its cap. ([#253](https://github.com/Kordal/bot-dot/issues/253))
