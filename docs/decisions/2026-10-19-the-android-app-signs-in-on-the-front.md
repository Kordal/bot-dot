2026-10-19: The Android app signs in on the front door's own page in a Custom Tab (`androidx.browser`) and gets its session through the Mac app's hand-off (`app: "android"`, a one-time 2-minute code at `botdot://signed-in`, exchanged with the secret whose SHA-256 the page carried at `POST /sign-in/handoff`), not through Credential Manager, because Credential Manager did not offer the owner's Proton Pass passkey in the app (there the provider must answer for the app's `android:apk-key-hash` origin, which needs `assetlinks.json` to name the app's key and the provider to honour it), while in the browser it answers for the site itself, on any domain and for any build. The secret stays because another app may claim `botdot://` and see the code. Credential Manager is removed rather than kept as a second way, since everything it signs in the browser signs in too; the front door still takes its origin from older apps. The page also shows "Back to the app", a link to the code's address, because Chrome may open no app from a script without a fresh tap once the passkey took a while.
