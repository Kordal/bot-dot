2026-10-09: The working line changes at most once a second per run, and the limit is the botdot server's: the first change goes out at once, later ones wait until the last one sent is a second old, and of those that waited only the newest is sent, so the last change always goes out. The feed, the run's stream and `workingLine` on the run all give the text that was sent last, so two apps never read differently. The step that comes with a bot's opening line does not replace it (the line announced that step), and a job's run starts as "Running <job's name>". A change is sent to who follows the run and to the feed and is never written to the run's log, which holds the tool calls the words are made from. Rejected: a limit in each app (two clocks, two results); sending every change and letting a slow line flicker; putting the newest text on the run before it was sent (a reload would read ahead of the feed).
