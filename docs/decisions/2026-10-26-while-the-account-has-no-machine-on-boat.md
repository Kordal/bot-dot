2026-10-26: While the account has no machine on boat.dev, the first bot whose own machine moves there gives that machine to the account (#584, `handOver` in `lib/boat.js`): its home goes into `/home/user/workspace/<bot>`, its browser profile becomes the account's `account` profile, and the other bots' files are copied into it as ADR 096 says. An account already at boat.dev's limit of machines at once (each bot running one of its own) could never get a new one. A move boat.dev refused with `limit_reached` is tried again by the one-minute tick 10 minutes after it failed. A bot's old machine is still stopped, not deleted: boat.dev does not count a stopped machine against the limit, and its disk stays a way back for 14 days. Rejected: deleting the old machines (`DELETE /sandboxes/{id}`), which cannot be undone and frees nothing the limit counts.
