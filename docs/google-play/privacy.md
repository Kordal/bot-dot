# botdot for Android: privacy policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026. This is the privacy policy of the botdot app for Android (`dev.botdot.app`) as published on Google Play and on GitHub.

## What the app is

botdot is a window onto a botdot server: software you, or someone you trust, run on your own computer or rented server ([github.com/Kordal/bot-dot](https://github.com/Kordal/bot-dot)). The app shows your bots, their chats and your Board, and sends what you type and share to that server. It holds no account of its own with the app's developer.

## What the app sends, and where

- **Only to the botdot server you sign in to.** Your messages to bots and the Chief of Staff, the files, photos and text you share or attach, your answers on cards, and your settings go to that server, and its replies, files and notifications come back from it. The address is the one you type or scan when you connect.
- **Sign-in** happens on the server's own page in your browser with a passkey. The app keeps the session it gets back on the phone. It never sees the passkey.
- **Nothing goes to the developer.** The app has no analytics, advertising, crash reporting or tracking of any kind, and no third-party software that sends data anywhere.
- **The server's admin** (you, on your own server, or the person who runs a machine of several accounts) can technically reach everything in the accounts on that machine, as its sign-up page says. What the server then does with your data, including what your bots send to Claude (Anthropic) and to the services you connect, is set by that server and the bots you make there, not by this app.

## What stays on the phone

The session, the server's address, your choice of light or dark and similar settings, and a cache of pictures and files you opened. They are removed when you sign out or uninstall the app. Android's backup of the app is off.

## Permissions

- **Internet and network state**: to reach your server and to reconnect when the network changes.
- **Notifications**: to show your bots' new replies and what needs you.
- **Foreground service and start at boot**: to keep the connection to your server open, so notifications arrive without a push service of Google or the developer. It shows as an ongoing notification.

## Deleting your data

Your data lives on the botdot server, not with the developer. To delete your account and all its data, delete it on your server, or ask the admin of the machine your account is on: they delete it in the admin page, which erases the account's data, bots and files. See [Deleting your botdot account](delete-account.md).

## Children

The app is not meant for children under 18.

## Changes and contact

A change to this policy is published here with a new date. Questions: the developer contact on the app's Google Play page, or an issue at [github.com/Kordal/bot-dot/issues](https://github.com/Kordal/bot-dot/issues).
