## Full description (4000): 2080

```
botdot is the Android app for botdot, open source software that gives you a team of AI bots you run by chat.

You don't set bots up by hand. You tell the Chief of Staff what you need, and it makes a bot for the job, runs it and reports back. Each bot has one job, its own instructions, tools, permissions, files and schedule, and a list of what it never does.

What you can do in the app
• Chat with each bot and with the Chief of Staff. Share files, photos and text into a chat from any app.
• Needs you: a script, a login or anything risky waits for your OK, showing exactly what it would run. Approve or decline in the app or right from the notification.
• The Board: live values your bots keep up to date, such as unread mail, your next meeting or a room's temperature, with switches and lists beside them.
• Groups: put two to six bots in one chat. Write once, and they answer and hand the work on to each other.
• Projects: one goal, a team of bots with a lead, a team chat, goals you can count and a calendar of to-dos.
• Pages: reports a bot keeps up to date on a schedule, with live numbers and charts.
• The bots' computer: each bot that uses it has its own screen and browser there, on your server or on an external virtual PC.
• Notifications when a bot replies, finishes or needs you, sent straight from your botdot, with no push service in between.

You need a botdot account
The app connects to a botdot server and shows nothing without one. Install botdot on your own Linux server or VM with one command (open source, MIT licence: github.com/ladrok/bot-dot), use an account the admin of a botdot server gives you, or have botdot hosted for you at botdot.app.

Your AI account
Every bot runs on Claude Code. Each account signs in with its own Claude subscription or Anthropic API key, or uses another model provider it chooses. botdot is an independent open source project, not made by or affiliated with Anthropic.

Privacy
The app talks only to the botdot server you sign in to. No analytics, no ads, no tracking. Sign-in uses a passkey on your server's own page.
```
