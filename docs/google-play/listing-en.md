## Full description (4000)

```
botdot is the Android app for your botdot server: a self-hosted team of AI bots built on Claude Code, which you run by chat.

You don't configure bots by hand. You tell the Chief of Staff what you need, and it makes the bots, runs them and reports back. Each bot has one job, its own tools, permissions, workspace and schedule.

What you can do in the app
• Chat with each bot and with the Chief of Staff, and share files, photos and text into a chat from any app.
• See what needs you: approve or decline what a bot asks before it acts, from the app or right from the notification.
• Watch your Board: live values your bots keep up to date, such as unread mail, your next meeting or a room's temperature.
• Put two to six bots in a group: write once, and they answer and hand the work on to each other.
• Projects: a team of bots, their files and their chat, in one place.
• Get a notification when a bot replies, finishes or needs you, straight from your own server, with no push service in between.

You need a botdot server
The app connects to your own botdot server and shows nothing without one. botdot is free and open source (MIT): install it on a Linux server or VM with one command, reach it over your own domain or a Tailscale tailnet, and sign in with your own Claude subscription or API key. Instructions: github.com/Kordal/bot-dot

Private by design
The app talks only to the server you sign in to. No analytics, no ads, no tracking. Sign-in uses a passkey on your server's own page.
```
