# ADR-271: An account switches to a new version when it is idle, at the time its owner chose

- **Status:** Accepted (2026-11-25, the owner's decisions in [#1686](https://github.com/ladrok/bot-dot/issues/1686))
- **Owning task:** [#1686](https://github.com/ladrok/bot-dot/issues/1686); this ADR comes with its first ticket, [#1687](https://github.com/ladrok/bot-dot/issues/1687), which builds the setting
- **Amends:** [ADR 053](053-each-account-owner-chooses-when-their-account-switches-to-a-new-version.md) (the owner's on/off becomes a "when", and the admin's Update only downloads) and [ADR 183](183-a-machine-of-one-account-updates-itself-at-night.md) (a machine of one account updates in two steps, by the same rule as every account)
- **Keeps:** [ADR 044](044-an-account-whose-new-server-doesnt-start-goes-back-and-the-others-carry-on.md)'s way back, [ADR 045](045-the-update-runs-in-phases-of-its-own-apart-from-the-install.md)'s phases and [ADR 155](155-an-update-can-name-its-version-and-keeps-a-snapshot-of-each-accounts-data.md)'s snapshot before a switch; [ADR 053](053-each-account-owner-chooses-when-their-account-switches-to-a-new-version.md)'s deadline and its one-version-behind limit

## Context

ADR 053 let an owner say "not now" to the admin's update, with one switch: "Update automatically". Anything else still restarts an account in the middle of its bots' work: the admin's Update switches every account that updates automatically at once, and a machine of one account (ADR 183) restarts itself at 04:00 whatever its bots are doing. A run a restart cuts off carries on (ADR 140), but the owner still loses the thing they were waiting for, and nobody tells them an update came.

What the owner wants is that an update reaches them as part of their day: said in their chat, moved to when nothing of theirs is running, at a time they chose.

## Decision

- **A "when" per account, instead of on/off.** `updateWhen` is `ready` ("As soon as it's ready"), `night` ("At night, when idle", the default) or `manual` ("When I say"). The front door keeps it in the account's entry of `front-door.json`, and only the account's owner changes it, in Settings on the web page and in the Android app, on a machine of several accounts and, with [#1691](https://github.com/ladrok/bot-dot/issues/1691), on a machine of one account. **No field means `night`; `autoUpdate: false` from before reads as `manual`.** `GET /front-door/update` says `when` (the apps' check that the front door has it) and keeps `autoUpdate` (`when !== "manual"`) for older apps; `POST /front-door/update` takes `{ when }` or the old `{ autoUpdate }` (`true` is `night`, `false` is `manual`).
- **The admin's "Update every account" only downloads.** It restarts no account: every account then moves by its own "when", at an idle moment. (Amends ADR 053, whose admin's Update switched every account that updated automatically.) An admin who updates again while an account still waits has root's step switch that account, never two versions behind (ADR 053), without an idle check: the admin's own act.
- **Idle.** An account moves to the new version only when it is idle: no run of a bot, group, Chief turn, look, checkup or script job going, nothing queued, no job, reminder or look due in the next 30 minutes, and no message from the owner in the last 10 minutes. Busy: the front door tries again later, never forces.
- **Night** is from 04:00 until the end of the owner's quiet hours (07:00 when they have none), in their quiet hours' time zone. Busy all night: the next night. (Settled with [#1689](https://github.com/ladrok/bot-dot/issues/1689): quiet hours that end at or before 04:00, or that do not cover 04:00 (13:00 to 15:00, 20:00 to 23:00), leave no night, so they count as none: 07:00; and so do quiet hours whose start is their end, in which no hour is quiet, and an account that doesn't answer, whose time zone is then the machine's.)
- **The deadline stays** (ADR 053): from the deadline on, the switch no longer waits for the owner's choice, only for an idle moment, for at most 24 hours; then it switches anyway (runs it cuts off carry on, ADR 140). On a machine of one account the deadline is 7 days.
- **"Tonight" is kept for one version.** The owner's "Tonight" (`POST /front-door/update { plan: "night" }`, from the card of [#1690](https://github.com/ladrok/bot-dot/issues/1690)) is kept in the account's entry of `front-door.json` (`updatePlan`) for the version that waits, and makes that version move as `night` does, whatever the owner's "when"; a later version waits for the owner again.
- **"Now" means now.** The owner's "Now" switches at once; the warning that work still running stops shows only when the account is busy.
- **A machine of one account updates in two steps** (amends ADR 183). The front door asks root's step to download (at 04:00, or as soon as it finds the release for "As soon as it's ready"); the account is left waiting; then the front door asks its switch by the same rule as every account. The split is also what lets the What's new notes be shown before the switch.
- **The front door decides every switch; root's step only carries them out.** For root's step every account is written `autoUpdate: false`, so root's step always leaves it waiting, and the front door asks each switch with `SWITCH-<name>` (ADR 053). `install.sh` needs no change. Only the front door knows the owner's "when", their quiet hours' time zone and the deadline; root's step would need each of them, read every minute with the image's Node.
- **The account's server answers "am I idle"** on a route only the front door reaches (the socket mark and `x-botdot-room`, as the group router's and the backups'): `GET /api/update/idle`. The front door asks it, and decides.
- **How the front door plans.** Every minute (and at a look of an owner or the admin, for what needs no question: an account a day past its deadline) the front door asks each account that waits what `lib/front-door/switch-plan.js` says. It asks the account's botdot server only what the plan needs: its settings, for the owner's quiet hours' time zone, when the owner's choice is `night`, and its idle answer. It uses the account's own `idle` verdict and recomputes nothing. An account that doesn't answer, or is older than the route, counts as busy; but not past the grace of 24 hours after the deadline. It asks for nothing while an update is asked or going (root's step does its part first), never twice within 15 minutes for one wait, and logs and audits (`update-switch`) each switch it asks, with why.
- **The announcement is a fixed botdot note plus a card**, not a Chief-written message. The front door tells each account its update state on a change and every minute (as it tells accounts what the off-site backups did); the account keeps one `update` card in Needs you, with **[Now]** and **[Tonight]**, and posts the note once per version into the Chief's chat with `chief.post()`, naming the card (ADR 252). The note lists the What's new titles; it is not an AI turn, not counted in the Chief's daily messages and held through quiet hours. The card's buttons call the front door's own routes with the owner's session, never the account server. ([#1690](https://github.com/ladrok/bot-dot/issues/1690), as built: the front door tells `POST /api/update/offer` `{ state: ready|moving|none, version, from, when, plan, deadline, notes, busy? }`, which only the front door reaches; `busy` is the account's own idle answer, absent when the front door could not ask, and is also in `GET /front-door/update`, so "Now" asks first only when it is `true`. The card is `kind: "update"` with `act` and `acts` ([Now] and, only for `manual` with no plan, [Tonight]), is drawn only for an app that asks for it by name (`?update=1`), has no Not now and no `switchAt`, and on `moving` says so with no button (`update-moving`); on `none` it goes. The card never rings: it is not a reason for a notification, so quiet hours hold. Answering it on the account's server is `409`.)
- **Migration.** Each account is `night` unless its entry says otherwise: an entry with `autoUpdate: false` reads as `manual`, none as `night`. From [#1689](https://github.com/ladrok/bot-dot/issues/1689) every entry of a machine where the front door plans the switches (see Consequences) is written with `updateWhen` in words and with `autoUpdate: false`, for root's step to read, and elsewhere with `autoUpdate: false` for `manual` alone, as before: when the front door starts for the entries it has, and whenever it saves for the others. The words are written first, so the old `autoUpdate: false` is never taken for `manual` by what it wrote itself.

## Alternatives

- **Root's step checking idle.** It would need to ask each account's server, and know the owner's "when", their quiet hours and the deadline, all of which are the front door's; and it would hold the installer's lock while it waits. Root's step only carries out a switch it is asked for.
- **The account's server telling the front door the owner's choice.** The front door is the one that reaches root's step and keeps the deadline; making it ask the server for the choice would make an account's own server a part of the switch, and a server that is down could never be switched. The front door keeps the choice, and the account's server is asked one question: is it idle.
- **`[[choices]]` in chat for Now and Tonight.** A choice in chat is an answer to a turn; the update is not one. A card in Needs you is where a question the owner can answer at any time already stands (ADR 252).
- **An AI-written announcement.** It costs a turn, is counted in the Chief's daily messages, may say what is not so and is not held for the owner's quiet hours. The note is fixed and lists the titles of What's new.
- **A one-step night update.** Download and switch in one go at 04:00, as ADR 183 did. The owner would not see the notes before the switch, and a busy account would have nowhere to wait.

## Consequences

- An update no longer restarts an account in the middle of its owner's day: it moves when idle, at night by default, or when the owner says.
- An account can run the version before the front door's for up to the deadline in normal use (ADR 053), and a machine of one account now can too: its `update.json` and status files say so.
- The admin's Update only downloads, from [#1689](https://github.com/ladrok/bot-dot/issues/1689): root's step leaves every account waiting, and the front door takes every switch over. The first update to this version is still run by the front door before it, whose data has no `autoUpdate: false` for the accounts that were not `manual`: root's step switches those as before, once.
- **Only where the front door plans the switches is every account pinned.** The front door plans only where its owner chooses (`plansSwitches`: root's step takes requests, the machine is not managed, and it has one account or an admin). On a managed machine, one account or several (no admin: the fleet console is), and on a machine of several accounts that names no admin, no one asks for a switch, so `autoUpdate: false` for every account would leave every account waiting for ever: no idle switch, no deadline, no "Now". There the old rule stays, `autoUpdate: false` for `manual` alone, and root's step switches the others as before. The front door passes the decision to its store, and the start-time migration follows it both ways: it pins every account where the front door plans, and lets them go where a machine's mode no longer does.
- An account whose botdot server is older than `GET /api/update/idle` ([#1688](https://github.com/ladrok/bot-dot/issues/1688)) cannot answer it: on the first update to a version that has the route, such an account counts as busy and waits for its owner's "Now", or for the deadline and 24 hours more (8 days). Not forced earlier on purpose: a cut-off run is the worse mistake.
- An app that doesn't know `when` shows "Update automatically" from `autoUpdate`, and turning it on there sets `night`.
- **The card appears only for updates from this version on.** The account's running server must have `POST /api/update/offer`, so an account moving from an older version is told nothing; the first update to a version that has the route is the last one without the card, and the owner then uses Settings or "Now" there.
- Out of scope, later: keeping the data snapshot longer for an owner's "Go back", a check after the switch, one update state for every app, and the desktop app's dialog.
- **`updates` is a separate word from planning.** The front door plans a machine of one account's switches from [#1689](https://github.com/ladrok/bot-dot/issues/1689) and `GET`/`POST /front-door/update` answer there, but `updates` in `/front-door/session` stays `false` on it until [#1691](https://github.com/ladrok/bot-dot/issues/1691), so the apps draw no Updates card whose words ("the admin can see…") are wrong for one account.
