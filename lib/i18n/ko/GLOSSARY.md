# Korean words

The Korean glossary: [lib/i18n/GLOSSARY.md](../GLOSSARY.md) says what each English term is in botdot. The same words as the What's new notes in `whats-new/ko/` and the Play listing (`docs/google-play/listing-ko.md`).

- **How the owner is addressed:** the polite 해요체 that KakaoTalk, Toss and Naver use ("저장했어요", "다시 시도해 주세요"), never 합니다체 and never 반말. "You" is left out, as Korean apps do; where it must be said, "내" ("내 봇", "내 계정"), never "당신".
- **Buttons and menu items:** a short noun or 하기 noun, no verb ending: 저장, 삭제, 취소, 보내기, 다시 연결, 문서로 열기. A switch's label is a noun phrase too (자동 업데이트).
- **Headings:** a noun (설정, 확인 필요, 예약 작업).
- **Short notes:** a past or state form without a subject: 저장됨, 복사됨, 연결됨.
- **A placeholder followed by a particle:** a {name} may end in a vowel or a consonant, so the particle is written with both forms (을(를), 이(가), 은(는), 와(과), (으)로) or the sentence is turned so that no particle follows it ("{name}: 완료", "{name} 봇에게").
- **Names stay as they are:** botdot (always lowercase), Claude, Claude Code, Gmail, Composio, boat.dev, Bitwarden, Haiku, Sonnet, Opus, GitHub, Google Play, Android, iPhone, Linux, Windows, Mac.
