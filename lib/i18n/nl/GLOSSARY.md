# Dutch words

The Dutch glossary: [lib/i18n/GLOSSARY.md](../GLOSSARY.md) says what each English term is in botdot. The same words go in the What's new notes in `whats-new/nl/` and the Play listing (`docs/google-play/listing-nl.md`). Dutch that reads naturally in the Netherlands and in Flanders.

- **You:** informal "je/jij/jouw", lowercase, as Dutch apps do ("Je bot is klaar"). "je" in running text, "jij/jou" only for stress ("Wacht op jou").
- **Buttons and menu items:** the infinitive, as Dutch apps do ("Opslaan", "Annuleren", "Verwijderen", "Stoppen"). Headings are nouns ("Instellingen", "Taken").
- **Names stay as they are:** botdot (always lowercase), Claude, Claude Code, Gmail, Composio, boat.dev, Bitwarden, Haiku, Sonnet, Opus, GitHub, Google Play. Compounds with a name take a hyphen: "Gmail-account", "Android-app".
- **English loanwords** Dutch apps use stay: app, chat, bot, update, account, team, project, stand-up, script, helpers.

## Words kept apart

- **opdracht** (a run's task) and **taak** (a job): never swapped. A job's request is "de opdracht van de taak".
- **kaart** waits in Wacht op jou; **tegel** is on the Bord.
- **pagina** is a bot's page; botdot in a browser is "de browser", never "de webpagina".
- **koppelen** for a connected app (Koppelingen, Ontkoppelen, Opnieuw koppelen); **verbinden** for a network connection to the botdot server ("Geen verbinding").
- **verwijderen** deletes; **vergeten** ("forget") erases a note.
