# 简体中文 words

The Simplified Chinese glossary: [lib/i18n/GLOSSARY.md](../GLOSSARY.md) says what each English term is in botdot. The same words are used in the dictionary, in the What's new notes of `whats-new/zh-Hans/` and in the Play listing (`docs/google-play/listing-zh-Hans.md`).

- **The owner** is addressed with 你 (never 您), as WeChat, Feishu and DingTalk speak to their users.
- **A button or a menu item** is a short verb phrase with no "请": 保存, 删除, 立即更新, 重新连接. A heading or a screen's name is a noun: 设置, 新功能, 待你处理. A sentence to the owner reads as a person would say it, plain and short.
- **Punctuation:** full-width in Chinese text (，。：；？！（）“”、). A list in a sentence takes 、. Ellipsis is …… in a sentence and … on a button or a placeholder ("搜索…").
- **Spaces:** one space between Chinese and a Latin word, a name or a `{placeholder}` that stands for one (`在 Gmail 中`, `已运行 {name}`), and between a number and its measure word (`{n} 个机器人`, `3 天`); none next to full-width punctuation.
- **Mainland words:** 账号 (not 帐户), 设置, 应用, 网页版, 通知, 置顶, 群组, @提及.
- **Names stay as they are:** botdot (always lowercase), Claude, Claude Code, Gmail, Composio, boat.dev, Bitwarden, Haiku, Sonnet, Opus, GitHub, Google Play, Android, iPhone.
- **Plurals:** Chinese has one form, `other`: `{n} 个机器人`.
