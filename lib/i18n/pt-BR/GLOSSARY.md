# Brazilian Portuguese words

The Brazilian Portuguese glossary: [lib/i18n/GLOSSARY.md](../GLOSSARY.md) says what each English term is in botdot. The same words as the What's new notes in `whats-new/pt-BR/` and the Play listing (`docs/google-play/listing-pt-BR.md`).

- **The owner is "você"**, as Brazilian apps say it: friendly, never "o senhor", never "tu". Verbs in the third person ("Você tem 2 cartões", "Toque para abrir").
- **A button or menu item is an infinitive:** "Salvar", "Cancelar", "Excluir", "Enviar", as Android, iOS and Google apps in Brazil write them. A heading is a noun ("Configurações", "Novidades"). A state is an adjective or a gerund ("Trabalhando", "Salvo").
- **"App"** (masculine: "o app") for an app, as Brazilians say it; "aplicativo" only where a longer word reads better.
- **Short words from phone apps:** "Excluir" (delete, not "Deletar"), "Fixar"/"fixada" (pin, as in WhatsApp), "Compartilhar", "Copiar", "Colar", "Toque" (tap), "Notificações", "Entrar" (sign in), "Sair" (sign out).
- **Names stay as they are:** botdot (always lowercase), Claude, Claude Code, Gmail, Composio, boat.dev, Bitwarden, Haiku, Sonnet, Opus, GitHub, Google Play. "o botdot" where an article is needed.
- **Plurals:** the CLDR rule for `pt` puts 0 and 1 in `one` ("0 bot"); a sentence that can say "nenhum" for zero does so in the code's own string where it has one.
