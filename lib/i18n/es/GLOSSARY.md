# Spanish words

The Spanish glossary: [lib/i18n/GLOSSARY.md](../GLOSSARY.md) says what each English term is in botdot. The same words as the What's new notes in `whats-new/es/` and the Play listing (`docs/google-play/listing-es.md`).

- **Neutral Spanish**, read the same in Spain and in Latin America: no "vosotros" (the plural "you" is "ustedes", and is rarely needed), no words of one region only ("ordenador", "móvil", "celular": "computadora", "teléfono").
- **Informal "tú"**, as phone apps in Spanish speak: "tu bot", "te pide", "Escribe un mensaje". Lowercase "tú/tu/ti".
- **A button or a menu item is an infinitive**, as in Android and iOS in Spanish: "Guardar", "Cancelar", "Eliminar", "Permitir siempre". A prompt in a field is an imperative ("Escribe un mensaje…"). A heading is a noun, in sentence case: only the first word and names take a capital ("Caja fuerte", "Jefe de gabinete").
- **A bot is masculine** ("el bot", "listo", "inactivo"), whatever its name. A bot's name stays as it is; Spanish puts no endings on it.
- **Names stay as they are:** botdot (lowercase, "el botdot" never: just "botdot"), Claude, Claude Code, Gmail, Composio, boat.dev, Bitwarden, Haiku, Sonnet, Opus, GitHub, Google Play.
- **Quotes are « »** («Permitir siempre», «{name}»), whatever the English has.
- **"app"** (feminine, "la app", "las apps"), not "aplicación", where room is short; "aplicación" may stand in a longer sentence.
