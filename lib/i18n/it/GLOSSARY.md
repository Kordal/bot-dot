# Italian words

The Italian glossary: [lib/i18n/GLOSSARY.md](../GLOSSARY.md) says what each English term is in botdot. The same words go in the dictionary, in the What's new notes of `whats-new/it/` and in the Play listing (`docs/google-play/listing-it.md`).

- **The owner is "tu"**, as Italian apps speak: "Salva le modifiche", "Il tuo bot", "ti chiede". Pronouns lowercase ("tuo", "te").
- **A button or a menu item is an imperative of "tu"**: "Salva", "Annulla", "Elimina", "Apri", "Riprova", "Consenti sempre". A heading is a noun ("Impostazioni", "Novità"). A state is an adjective or a past participle ("Salvato", "Fatto").
- **Names stay as they are:** botdot (lowercase, masculine: "il botdot", rarely needed), Claude, Claude Code, Gmail, Composio, boat.dev, Bitwarden, Haiku, Sonnet, Opus, GitHub, Google Play.
- **English words Italians use in apps stay English:** bot (invariable: "il bot", "i bot"), team, file, app, link, password, chat, stand-up, check-up, login, token, browser, desktop, email.
- Plurals: Italian has `one`, `many` (only for round millions, "1.000.000 di bot") and `other`. `many` takes the `other` text with "di" where it reads better; in practice the same text as `other`.

A bot's working line speaks in the first person present, as Polish does: "Apro {host}", "Leggo un file", "Cerco". An error is "Impossibile …" + infinitive ("Impossibile salvare"); a quote is «…».

Common buttons: Salva, Annulla, Elimina, Modifica, Invia, Riprova, Chiudi, Indietro, Apri, Copia, Rinomina, Aggiungi, Rimuovi, Pausa / Riprendi, Collega, Accedi, Esci, Approva, Fissa / Rimuovi dai fissati, Mostra / Nascondi, Carica, Scarica, Continua, Fine.
