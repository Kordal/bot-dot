# Polish words

The Polish glossary: [lib/i18n/GLOSSARY.md](../GLOSSARY.md) says what each English term is in botdot. The same words as the What's new notes in `whats-new/pl/` and the Play listing (`docs/google-play/listing-pl.md`). Informal "ty", lowercase ("twoje", "ciebie"). Names stay as they are: botdot, Claude, Gmail, Composio, boat.dev, Haiku.
